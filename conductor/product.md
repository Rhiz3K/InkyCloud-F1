# Product Guide - F1 E-Ink Calendar

## Initial Concept
Free F1 race calendar for your E-Ink display! Optimized for 800x480 1-bit BMP displays (LaskaKit).

## Vision
To provide F1 enthusiasts with a beautiful, informative, and maintenance-free way to track the Grand Prix season on their E-Ink devices. The project aims to bridge the gap between complex racing data and low-power display technology, offering a "glanceable" dashboard for the race weekend.

## Target Audience
- **F1 Enthusiasts:** Fans who want to keep track of race times and circuit details at a glance.
- **E-Ink Device Owners:** Users of LaskaKit, Waveshare, or other ePaper displays looking for high-quality, functional content.

## Core Value Propositions
- **High-Quality Rendering:** Images are specifically optimized for 1-bit (black and white) displays to ensure crisp text and clear graphics.
- **Informative Layout:** Beyond simple details, the calendar provides track maps, historical podium results, and full session schedules.
- **Set-and-Forget:** Designed to be always up-to-date with minimal user intervention.

## Key Features
- **Informative Dashboard:** 800x480 BMP generation including circuit maps, lap counts, and historical data.
- **Session Tracking:** Complete schedule for FP1, FP2, FP3, Qualifying, Sprint, and Race times.
- **Multi-language Support:** Currently supports English and Czech.
- **Timezone Awareness:** Automatic conversion of race times to the user's local timezone.

## Roadmap
- **Color Support:** Expansion to 3-color (Red/Black/White) E-Ink displays to match the F1 brand aesthetic.
- **Layout Expansion:** Support for varied display sizes (4.2", 5.83", 12.48") and oriented views.
